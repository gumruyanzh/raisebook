# Code of Conduct

Raisebook is built by founders and developers who want a useful tool to stay free. Everyone
taking part in the project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project
space) agrees to keep it a place where people can do good work together.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Keep discussions on topic and specific.
- Accept a decision once it is made, and reopen it with new facts, not volume.
- Protect privacy: never post anyone's personal data, including real investor or founder
  details, contact information, API keys or private messages.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Spam, promotion, or using the project to solicit investors or founders.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks it, depending on what
happened and whether it happened before.

Report a problem to 0@raisebook.org. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is not shared without their consent. Maintainers who are involved in a report step
away from handling it.

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
